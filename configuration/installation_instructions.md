Dexra checks Solana token launches, holders, wallets, pools, perp markets and swaps before your agent trades, and answers each with a verdict.

The free tools (`describe_address`, `service_stats`, `score_performance`) work without a key. For the paid tools, either pay per call in USDC over x402, or:

1. Create an account at [dexra.dev](https://dexra.dev) (100 free credits a day once your email is confirmed, or a subscription).
2. Create an API key at [dexra.dev/app/keys](https://dexra.dev/app/keys).
3. Paste it as `api_key`.
